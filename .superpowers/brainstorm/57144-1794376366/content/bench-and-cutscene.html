Alchemy bench: layout preview

A rough moving sketch of the two things you'd look at most. It shows layout and mood only; the Unity version would have finer art. Names and numbers are placeholders.

1. The bench (where you play)

  • Centre: the 3×3 bench. Materials on neighbouring tiles react; glowing links show which pairs will fire.
  • Bottom: your hand, a few materials drawn this round. Drag one onto a tile.
  • Top left: how deep you are and the potency needed to reach the next depth.
  • Top right: catalysts (permanent rule-benders), trials left before the run ends, and redraws.
  • Right: the journal of discoveries and the Ignite button that commits the experiment.
  • Left: the last reaction, plus a half-hidden hint earned from a failed mix.

2. The reaction cutscene (plays when you press Ignite)

This loops a 5-second example: materials are drawn into the vessel, a flash, then the reveal with the name and the potency counting up. A first-time discovery gets the full sequence; a reaction you've already seen plays a one-second short version. Click skips it.
