Athanor: the opening cutscene

The storyboard in docs/devlogs/phase-4-lore.md, played at 640x360 with the game's art, fonts and timings. Click the picture as in the game: a click finishes the line being written, the next ends the wait, a click during a scroll or a walk jumps to its end. Change any choice on the right and the playback follows at once.

Placeholder art, drawn in code for this mockup only: the stair (cellar_stair), the Keepers (keeper_walk, keeper_kneel, keeper_last), cellar_bottom, roots_grow and the "new blank book" option. Judge their placement, size and motion, not their drawing. Everything else is the game's own PNGs. Lighting, embers and glows are approximations of the game's Light2D and bloom.
