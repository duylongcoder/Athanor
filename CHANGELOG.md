# Changelog

Player-facing notes for each release of Athanor. Newest first.

## DRAFT - Phase 5a (version not bumped; owner sets it)

Status: DRAFT, not published. Build date 2026-11-16.

### New
- **Settings.** A new Settings button on the title screen: choose the language and vibration. Reset progress has moved in here.
- **Vietnamese.** The whole game can now be played in Vietnamese, with a font that shows Vietnamese text cleanly. On first launch the game follows your browser's language.
- **Pause menu.** Pause a run with the button on the HUD or Esc: resume, turn vibration on or off, or quit to the title. Quitting ends the run without counting it; your journal is kept.
- **Tutorial: skip and "How to play".** If you already have journal entries you can skip the tutorial, and "How to play" on the title plays it again without touching your save. "Watch intro" replays the story.
- **Rarity stars.** Starting materials, catalyst offers and your hand show stars for how rare they are; the Journal shows them once you have discovered the material.
- **Depth intros and window views.** Each depth opens with a short intro line under its name, and the cellar window shows a view for the depth you are on.
- **Won screen.** After a win the summary shows where you planted the seed: "Planted at depth N".
- **Rotate your phone.** On a phone held upright, a message asks you to turn it sideways.
- **New effects.** New effects when you collect a result, when a new reaction is discovered, and when a pair does nothing.
- **Vibration on Android.** A short pulse on a discovery, a cleared depth and the end of a run (can be turned off).

### Changed
- **Smaller download.** The game downloads at 10.2 MiB, about 52 KB smaller than before these changes.
